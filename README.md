I'm a data analytics trainer and mentor, currently pursuing a **B.Tech in Computer Science (AI)**, and I work at **ORIX India**. My focus is simple: make analytics practical, and make learners employable.

- 📊 I teach **Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI** through real business problems, not toy datasets

- 🕵️ Founder of **Data Detective Club**, a community for hands-on, data-driven problem solving
- ✍️ I write about analytics concepts and career guidance on [Medium](https://medium.com/@adityakalyanbscc)
